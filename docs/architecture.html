Architecture · Family Sudoku

How Family Sudoku fits together

One HTML file on the device does all the work. The cloud only serves that file and keeps a family's players in step across their phones and the shared iPad.

The headline

One file, one state

Every screen reads the same game state: the board, the scoring rules, the players and their history. A tap changes the state, the state is saved on the device at once, and the page re-renders from it. Tailwind and Chart.js are compiled into the file, so nothing is fetched while playing.

Two engines, one source

The engine runs twice

The engine script holds the solver, the grader and twelve human techniques from naked singles to XY-Chain. On the page it answers hints and grades paper puzzles. Its text is also loaded into a Web Worker, which builds boards and pre-builds the next one, so a Grandmaster carve never freezes the screen.

Kids' grids (4×4, 6×6, 8×8) use a small generator on the page; they take milliseconds.

Local first

Sync is a copy, not a login

Progress lives in the browser and works offline. With a family code, the players are also written to one Firestore document, families/{code}, a second and a half after any change and read back on open. Each player carries a timestamp and the newer copy wins, so two people can play on two devices at once.
