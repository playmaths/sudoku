Scoring: a hint comes in three taps (look here, explain, apply) and each tap costs a third of a hint; a whole hint adds 15s on Easy, 30s Medium, 45s Tricky, 60s Hard, 90s Master, 120s Grandmaster, 150s Extreme. A wrong digit (with red marking on) adds 15s Medium, 20s Tricky, 30s Hard, 60s Master, 90s Grandmaster, 120s Extreme, and a third wrong digit on Hard or above ends the board. Extreme boards need techniques the coach does not teach, so at some point its only help is to reveal a square. Turn red marking off for a Purist solve: no checks, no penalties, 👑 if it's right.

Kids mode: no timer on screen, no penalties. A wrong number bounces back and shows where it clashes. Help is free. Every finished board earns a sticker.

Players & devices

Players & Device Sync

Everyone who plays has their own name, icon, boards and stats, and can change the name or icon here at any time. Switch players with a tap on the name in the header.

Players

Family code · same players on every device

Tap New family code on the first device, then type that code on every other phone or tablet. From then on each player's boards, streaks and stats follow them to any device, and two people can play on two devices at once. No account, no sign-in.

Manual backup (no internet needed)

Copies every profile and in-progress board as one long code to paste on another device. Loading a code replaces what that device had.

Zero Ads Forever

Pin to iPad Home Screen & GitHub Hosting

1

One Link for the Whole Family

The game lives at playmaths.github.io/sudoku. It is a single page with no account, no ads and no tracking, and once opened it keeps working without an internet connection.

2

Pin It as an App on Their iPad & iPhones

Open the link in Safari on their iPad or iPhone, tap the Share button (square with upward arrow), and tap "Add to Home Screen". It launches full-screen like a native App Store game—with zero ads or popups.

3

Your Progress Follows You

Progress is saved on the device straight away and works offline. With a family code, every device that knows the code keeps the same players: finish a board on the iPad and the stats are on your phone a moment later. The newer copy of each player always wins, so two people can play at the same time on two devices.
